# Zomato Product Teardown

**Presented by:** Team Product Saviours (Rudra Baldaniya, Varun Singhal, Jones G)[cite: 1]  
**Organization:** 180 Degrees Consulting, BIT Mesra[cite: 1]  

## Project Overview
This presentation analyzes Zomato's three-sided marketplace, focusing on how the platform manages demand aggregation, kitchen utilization, and rider logistics[cite: 1]. With the food delivery market projected to grow from $45.15 Bn in 2024 to $192.31 Bn by 2031, Zomato is currently transitioning into an optimization phase prioritized around operational stability and unit economics[cite: 1].

## Target Demographics
* **Young Professionals (25-34):** The largest user segment, driven heavily by time scarcity and habitual ordering[cite: 1].
* **Students (18-24):** The second-largest segment, defined by high frequency usage and price sensitivity[cite: 1].
* **Families & Older Demographics (35+):** Usage drops significantly in these segments due to a preference for home-cooked meals and lower risk tolerance[cite: 1].

## Core Operational & UX Challenges
* **Decision Overload:** The most significant drop-off in the user journey occurs before the cart phase because users struggle with too many options[cite: 1].
* **Psychological Price Pain:** Users are frustrated by unpredictable final costs and opaque delivery fees at checkout[cite: 1].
* **Hidden Time Costs:** Getting a single meal carries a 30-45 minute time tax due to decision-making, prep time, and delivery waiting[cite: 1].
* **Supply Imbalance:** Rider availability fluctuates based on time and weather, often dropping when peak demand is highest[cite: 1].

## Proposed Product Fixes
* **Streamlined Checkout:** Auto-apply the single best coupon to reduce cart abandonment[cite: 1].
* **Pricing Transparency:** Implement an upfront 'Final Payable' pricing model to eliminate psychological price pain[cite: 1].
* **Enhanced Trust:** Introduce a visible Restaurant Reliability Score and show SLA tracking for preparation times to reduce user risk[cite: 1].
* **Rider Efficiency:** Utilize heatmap-triggered surges and dynamic payouts linked to zone stress to balance rider supply[cite: 1].
